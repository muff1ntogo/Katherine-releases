Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy says what data the Katherine desktop app and this website use, where it is kept and how to have it removed. "We" means the developer of Katherine. Contact: the Katherine-releases issues page.

1. Data kept on your computer

  • Your Riot ID, region, rank, champion mastery and ranked match history, fetched from Riot Games' API.
  • Your champion pools, settings and plans.
  • The champion selects Katherine followed (picks, bans and results) and the same data for team-mates you add to your roster.

This stays in the app's data folder on your computer. Uninstalling the app and deleting that folder removes it. The app also has a "Delete my data" option in Settings.

2. Data kept on our server

  • Account: if you sign in, an account identifier from the sign-in provider and your Riot ID.
  • Groups: if you create or join a team or a duo, its membership and the pools, plans and logged drafts shared in it, so that every member sees the same.
  • Requests for Riot data: the app asks our server to fetch your Riot data, so that no API key is stored in the app. The server keeps short-lived copies to stay within Riot's limits. Its logs do not contain Riot IDs or player identifiers.

You can use Katherine without an account. Then nothing in the first two points is stored.

3. Champion statistics

The statistics Katherine shows are built from public ranked matches. They are stored and distributed as aggregate counts per champion, with no player identities.

4. What we do not do

  • No advertising, no analytics trackers and no sale or sharing of your data with third parties.
  • No usage telemetry or crash reports are sent from the app.
  • Katherine does not identify or look up players whose names the game hides.

5. This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies of ours. GitHub may log your IP address when you visit or download the installer; see GitHub's privacy statement.

6. How long data is kept

Account and group data are kept until you delete them or your account. Short-lived copies of Riot data on the server expire on their own. Data on your computer is kept until you delete it.

7. Your rights

You can see, correct or delete your data: in the app (Settings, "Delete my data" and "Delete my account") or by asking on the Katherine-releases issues page (do not post personal details there; we will arrange a private channel). We also act on deletion requests that Riot Games passes on to us. If you are in the EU or the UK you have the rights given by the GDPR, including the right to complain to your data protection authority.

8. Children

Katherine is for people old enough to hold a Riot account in their region.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes and the app will say so at its next start.
