Katherine is a free Windows assistant and coach for League of Legends ranked players, whether you queue alone, with a duo or as a team of five. It sits beside the League client and helps you think through champion select.

Download for Windows Free. No ads, no paid features. Windows 10 or 11.

Options in champion select

On your turn, Katherine shows several pick and ban options with the reasons behind each: the lane matchup, the team composition and your own champion pool. You decide. It never hovers, picks or locks for you.

Your own stats

Review your ranked history, your champion pool and how your lanes went compared with the average for your rank. Teams and duos who join the same group see the games they played together.

Strategy notes

Once the draft is locked, see notes for your matchup that are known before the game starts: the lane plan and the levels and items where each side is strong. An optional small window keeps them in view.

How it works

  1. Install Katherine and open it next to the League client.
  2. Tell it your Riot ID and the roles you play. It reads your ranked games and proposes a champion pool you can edit.
  3. Queue as usual. When champion select starts, Katherine follows the draft and shows its options on your turn.
  4. After the game, the draft and the result appear in your review pages.

What it does not do

  • It does not act in the League client. It only reads champion select.
  • It does not read the running game, and shows nothing during a game that was not known before it started.
  • It does not identify or look up players whose names the game hides.

Your data

Champion statistics come from ranked matches and are stored without player identities. Your own pools, games and drafts are kept on your computer, and are shared only with the group you choose to join. The full details are in the Privacy Policy.
